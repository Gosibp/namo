The Problem

Rising fast. Found too late. Out of reach.

Cancer touches nearly every Indian family — yet most cases are caught too late, and quality treatment is concentrated in a few big cities.

A growing burden
1.9 million

new cancer cases every year — and 1 in 9 Indians will face cancer in their lifetime.

Caught too late
7 in 10

cases are found at an advanced stage. Caught early, 9 in 10 patients survive — caught late, only 1 in 7 do.

Out of reach
95%

of cancer centres are in cities, while 7 in 10 Indians live in rural areas — where death rates are twice as high.

Sources: EY — Call for Action (2022); ICMR Indian Cancer Registry; WHO; American Cancer Society. Verify before publishing.

Our Plan · NAMO-CAN

A network that brings care closer to home

Four connected steps — awareness, early detection, treatment, and follow-up — delivered through the National Mission of Oncology – Cancer Access Network.

Awareness

Community ambassadors, youth volunteers and theatre-based campaigns that bring cancer awareness into everyday life, district by district.

Early Detection & Screening

Regular screening close to where people live, so cancers are found early — when treatment works best and costs least.

A Network of Cancer Centres

Connected hospitals and cancer centres so quality treatment is available closer to home, not only in big cities.

Community & Follow-up Care

Staying with patients and families through treatment and recovery, with local volunteers who keep care going.

Citizen and youth participation

Every ambassador and youth volunteer we train gains a skill and a role in their community — so the mission also builds local livelihoods as it grows.

About

About the Foundation

How NAMO Cancer Care Foundation began, and the idea that holds it together.

“A sacred commitment to make cancer care accessible, affordable, and dignified for every Indian citizen, embodying the true spirit of Atmanirbhar Bharat. This mission heals the nation today and builds India’s sovereign oncology innovation ecosystem for tomorrow.”

NAMO Cancer Care Foundation grew from a simple conviction: that no one in India should face cancer late, alone, or far from help.

What we believe

Cancer care should follow the citizen, not the other way round. That means meeting people early, close to home, and staying with them through treatment and recovery — regardless of income or location.

Vision

Our vision

Where we are headed, and the future we are working towards — community by community.

Every Indian — wherever they live, whatever they earn — should be able to find quality cancer care close to home.

Our vision is holistic cancer care within reach of 15 crore citizens by 2030, built community by community, in step with India’s goal of a cancer-free future.

Care that follows the citizen

Bring awareness, screening, diagnosis and treatment to where people live — not the other way round — so no family has to travel far or wait long for care.

A connected network of centres

Build a pan-India network of cooperative cancer centres — shared standards, shared systems, and shared knowledge — so quality is consistent from a district town to a metro.

Sovereign oncology innovation

Nurture India’s own research, technology and talent in oncology — from AI-powered diagnostics to affordable therapies — as an Atmanirbhar Bharat commitment for the next generation.

Community & livelihoods

Grow a movement of citizen ambassadors and youth volunteers — giving them a skill, a role, and a livelihood as the mission expands into their districts.

Three principles at the heart of it

Early

Caught early, when treatment is most effective — through awareness and routine screening close to home.

Near

Treated with dignity at a connected network of centres, so quality care is never far from where people live.

Together

Supported at every stage by local volunteers and ambassadors who keep care going through recovery.

An added impact: citizen and youth participation

Every ambassador and youth volunteer we train gains a skill and a role in their community — so the mission also builds local livelihoods as it grows.
