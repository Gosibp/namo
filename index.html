Questions, answered

Before you
join us.

Is treatment really 100% free for patients?

Yes. Three engines make it sustainable: Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY reimbursements as the baseline, philanthropy and CSR filling the gaps, and surpluses from industry-sponsored clinical research cross-subsidising care. Patients handle no bills and no paperwork.

Where will the first centres be built?

Phase one anchors seven comprehensive centres in the Moksha Kshetras — Ayodhya, Mathura, Haridwar, Varanasi, Ujjain, Dwarka and Kanchipuram. Ayodhya is already underway, with six acres of land gifted for a 150-bed hospital.

Why place cancer centres in sacred cities?

They are strategic healthcare anchors: population-proximal to 47 crore people in underserved heartland regions, reducing catastrophic travel and cost — while offering patients dignity, peace and holistic healing alongside world-class medicine.

Is NCCF a registered organisation?

Yes — NAMO Cancer Care Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit registered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat (CIN U86900GJ2025NPL171271), verifiable on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs portal.

How can I contribute?

Take the Swasthya Sankalp pledge, volunteer as a Swasthya Rakshak in your district, direct your company’s CSR, or donate — every contribution is tracked transparently. Write to info@namoccf.org and we’ll find your place in the mission.
